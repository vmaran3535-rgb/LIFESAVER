TAMIL NADU MONSOON READINESS

🌊 Flood Safety Mode

Practical preparedness guidance. This is not a live flood warning service.

Before heavy rain

Keep drinking water, essential medicines, torch and charged power bank ready.
Save important documents in a waterproof pouch.
Know your family's meeting point and local authority shelter information.

During flooding

Move to a safer higher place when authorities advise.
Never walk or drive through floodwater; avoid drains and electrical wires.
Follow official instructions and call 112 for urgent help in India.

After water recedes

Wait for authorities to confirm an area is safe before returning.
Avoid contact with floodwater and damaged electrical equipment.
Use safe drinking water and follow public-health guidance.
